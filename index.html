Help around the house.

Our small language agent (SLA) is on kitchen duty. The 5M version is running right here in your browser. Watch it open cupboards, put things away and ask where something belongs when the instructions leave that part out.

How our SLA learned its chores

Gemma gave our SLA its first kitchen lessons through written demonstrations. Then we let the SLA practise and rewarded it for finishing the job. On 134 new chores, the version we trained this way averaged 74.4% success across three runs. Gemma completed 67.9% of the same chores.

Your personal shopper.

A pair of shoes can be the right colour and still be a size too small. Our 10M-parameter SLA runs in your browser. Watch it search a store of 1,000 products for a match, choose a size and decide what to buy.

How our SLA learned to shop

Our shopper learned from 22,005 decisions made by an expert model across 4,068 shopping requests. Then it tried the job for itself. The store gave partial credit for a near miss, but a purchase only counted as a success when it met every requirement.
